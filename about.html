Our Story

LUX is more than a brand. It's a movement born in the streets of Cairo, mixing 90s nostalgia with modern streetwear culture.

Born in Cairo

We started with one idea: to create Egyptian streetwear that could stand shoulder-to-shoulder with the global scene.

From the chaos of downtown Cairo to the calm of the Nile, our designs channel the energy of a city that never sleeps.

Made in Egypt

Every LUX piece is designed and manufactured locally, supporting Egyptian artisans.

We believe in quality you can feel and roots you can trust. No shortcuts, no compromises.

Our Values

Quality

Community
